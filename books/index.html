Classical Heritage Meets Computational Rigor

At 18 years old, Raghav Shukla represents a rare synthesis of modern technological acumen and deep reverence for ancient Indian literature. Currently pursuing his B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at IIT Bhilai (Bhilai) alongside his degree studies in Data Science at IIT Madras, Raghav approaches classical text preservation with mathematical exactitude.

As the founder of Taxtron (founded in 2024), documentary creator, and researcher, he has focused his efforts on computational typesetting, digitizing rare Sanskrit treatises, and providing clean, metrically precise trilingual formats for global readers and spiritual seekers.

"Preserving sacred Sanskrit literature is not merely about archiving words—it is about applying modern computational typography to ensure absolute phonetic clarity, metric fidelity, and timeless elegance for generations to come."

Shiva Stotrams of Adi Shankaracharya (Volume 1) is the culmination of painstaking editorial collation, cross-referencing authentic manuscript variants, and typesetting in high-density Devanagari typography along with Roman IAST transliteration and English verse exposition.
