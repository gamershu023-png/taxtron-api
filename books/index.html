Bridging Sacred Indian Heritage & Modern Technology

At 18 years old, Raghav Shukla is an author, documentarian/filmmaker, and technology researcher. He is currently pursuing his B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at IIT (Bhilai) alongside his degree studies in Data Science & Applications at IIT Madras.

As the founder of Taxtron (founded in 2024), Raghav fuses computational typesetting, digital archival techniques, and cinematic storytelling to restore and publish classical Sanskrit texts with absolute metric and phonetic clarity.

"Preserving ancient wisdom requires more than static digitizing—it demands high-precision typography, metric accuracy, and accessible multi-script translations to make eternal hymns vibrant for seekers worldwide."

Shiva Stotrams of Adi Shankaracharya (Volume 1) reflects his dedication to structural perfection, combining verified Devanagari manuscripts, standard Roman IAST transliteration, and lucid English translations.
